    About Our School

    Nurturing Generations Since 1996

    Established in 1996, Nalanda E-Techno School has been nurturing generations of students through quality education, discipline, innovation, and student-centered learning in Kadali, Malikipuram, Konaseema District, Andhra Pradesh.

    We are committed to providing value-based education that blends academic excellence with technology-enabled learning and holistic development for every student from Nursery to 10th Class.
